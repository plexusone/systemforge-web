# v0.10.0 Release Notes

**Release Date:** 2026-11-16

The organization console gets a Billing page: members see the
organization's plan, and owners and admins choose a plan through checkout
and manage it in the payment provider's billing portal. It is the page for
the billing API of SystemForge v0.33.0 and later. The other packages are
unchanged.

| Package              | Version   |
| -------------------- | --------- |
| `@plexusone/console` | 0.3.0     |
| every other package  | unchanged |

## Highlights

- **The organization's plan.** The current plan, the subscription's state
  (renewing, ending, past due or in a trial) and the deployment's plans
  side by side with their prices, limits and features, from the pricing
  view SystemForge serves.
- **Choosing a plan.** An owner or admin chooses a plan, monthly or
  yearly, and is sent to checkout; on return the page thanks them, or says
  nothing was charged. An organization that pays changes or cancels its
  plan, its card and its invoices in the billing portal. Other members see
  the plan and who can change it.
- **Free mode and test mode.** A deployment that takes no payments shows
  the plans with no way to buy, and says so. A test-mode key is flagged
  with "Test mode: no real charges".
- **Plans set by the operator.** When SystemForge reports `setByOperator`,
  the page says the operator set the plan and offers no other; the billing
  portal still manages a paid subscription.
- **Prices in any currency and interval.** Amounts are scaled by the
  currency's minor unit, so zero-decimal currencies such as JPY are shown
  correctly, and a plan with only a yearly price shows and sells it.
- **Opt in.** `createConsoleApp({ billing: true })` lists Billing in the
  navigation. The page is served at `/orgs/{id}/billing` either way, and a
  deployment without billing answers it with "Billing is not enabled".

The release adds no breaking change.

## Requirements

SystemForge v0.33.0 or later, with a `billing` section in SystemAuth's
configuration. See SystemForge's
[billing guide](https://plexusone.github.io/systemforge/guides/billing/).

See [@plexusone/console](../packages/console.md#billing).
